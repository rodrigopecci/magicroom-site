Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Magic Room ("we", "us") handles information when you play our mobile games, including Tile Match (the "Games"), and use this website. We keep data collection to what is needed to run the Games, show ads and process purchases.

1. Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account registration to play.

Game data stored on your device

Your progress, settings, coins, power-ups and purchase status are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this data.

Information collected by our partners

The Games use the third-party services below, which may collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and operating system information, app usage and ad interaction data, and diagnostic data. This is used to serve, limit, measure and, if you consent, personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Google Privacy Policy
  • Google User Messaging Platform shows consent forms where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states) and stores your choices on your device.
  • Apple App Store processes all in-app purchases. We never receive your payment card details. We receive transaction information (such as the product purchased and a transaction identifier) needed to deliver and restore your purchase. Apple Privacy Policy
  • Unity Technologies provides the game engine and the Unity In-App Purchasing service, which may process device identifiers and purchase events to operate purchases. Unity Privacy Policy

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and the content of your message, and use them only to answer you.

2. Tracking and personalized ads

On iOS, the Games ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before accessing your device's advertising identifier. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the identifier is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Where the law requires it, a consent form is shown before ads are personalized. Purchasing "No Ads" removes the ads shown between levels; optional rewarded videos remain available if you choose to watch them.

3. How information is used

  • To run the Games and save your progress on your device
  • To deliver and restore in-app purchases
  • To show ads, which keep the Games free to play
  • To respond to support requests
  • To prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Legal bases

Where the EU/UK GDPR or Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD) applies, we and our partners rely on your consent for personalized advertising and tracking, on the performance of a contract to provide the Games and your purchases, and on legitimate interests for non-personalized ads, security and fraud prevention.

5. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the Game. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request. Our partners retain data according to their own policies.

6. International transfers

Our partners may process information in countries other than yours, including the United States, using legally recognized safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Because we don't store your data on our servers, most choices are made directly on your device (tracking settings, consent forms, or deleting the Game). For data held by our partners, use their privacy controls linked above. For anything else, contact us and we'll help. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD).

8. Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, and we rely on established providers (Apple, Google, Unity) for payments and advertising. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always available on this page, with the effective date shown at the top.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: support@magicroomgames.com
